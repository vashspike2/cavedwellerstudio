Club It Up privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Club It Up is made by Cave Dweller Studio. This explains what the app knows about you, who can see it, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it.

What is collected

Your email address. Used to sign you in and to send you sign-in codes. Nothing else is sent to it, and other members never see it.

Your name and profile photo. The name you choose, and a photo if you add one, are shown to people who share a club with you. Nobody else can see them.

What you write and add to a club. Reviews and scores, board posts, replies, discussion messages, emoji reactions, and the topics, reviews and discussions you add. These are visible to the members of that club and to nobody else.

Photos you choose. Club icons, covers for topics and items, photos on board posts, and your profile photo. Each one is resized on your phone and saved as a new image before it is uploaded, which leaves behind the location and camera details the original photo carried. The app asks for access to your photo library only when you choose a photo, and reads only the photo you pick.

Role requests. If you ask for a higher role in a club, the request and any note you add are shown to that club's admins.

Who you block, and anything you report. Your block list is private to you. A report includes what you reported, why, and that you reported it, and is read only by Cave Dweller Studio.

Settings kept on your phone. Reading settings, which reviews you have read, your alert choices and your app icon choice stay on your device and are not uploaded.

Club It Up has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone for their own purposes.

Beta testing. If you use the TestFlight beta, Apple may pass along crash reports and any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

Where it is kept

A copy is kept on your device so screens open without waiting on the network. The shared copy lives in a Postgres database and private file storage hosted by Supabase in the United States. Photos are stored privately: only members of the club a photo belongs to can see it, and only people who share a club with you can see your profile photo.

Three other companies are involved, and only these three:

  • Supabase hosts the database, the sign-in service and stored photos.
  • Resend sends sign-in emails. It sees your email address.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) answers film and TV lookups when you search for a title. Today the app asks TMDB directly, so TMDB sees your device's network address along with what you typed. This is planned to move to our own server, which will stop that.

How long it is kept

Until you or your club delete it.

  • Replacing or removing your profile photo deletes the old one from storage.
  • Removing a photo from a post, or deleting a post, deletes its photos.
  • Deleting a club deletes the club, everything written in it, and its photos.

Deleting your account

Go to Me, then Delete my account, inside the app.

This removes your account, your profile photo, your membership of every club, your name, your emoji reactions, any role requests you made, and your settings. Reviews and posts you wrote stay in their clubs without your name on them, so other members do not lose their club's history. A club where you were the only member is deleted with you.

If you would rather everything you wrote was removed as well, write to the address below and ask.

Age

Club It Up is for people aged 13 and older. If you believe someone younger has an account, write to the address below and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. If a change affects what is collected or who can see it, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
